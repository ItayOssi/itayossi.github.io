החיבור ל-Google

SumIT מבקשת גישה רק לקבצים שהיא עצמה יוצרת ב-Drive שלך (ההרשאה drive.file), ולשם, למייל ולתמונה של החשבון. היא לא רואה את שאר הקבצים ב-Drive. אין ל-SumIT שרת משלה: הכל עובר ישירות בין המחשב שלך לבין Google. הפרטים המלאים במדיניות הפרטיות.

About SumIT

SumIT is a study app for macOS and Windows, made for students at HIT (Holon Institute of Technology). It brings the student's Moodle courses, Zoom recordings and CS24 materials into one place, and uses an AI engine that the student connects (Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT) to build a summary of each lecture with its homework explained.

Google sign-in. Students may sign in with Google to keep their study folder, tasks and profile in a "SumIT" folder of their own Google Drive and get them back on another computer. SumIT asks only for the drive.file scope (the files it creates) and the account's name, email and picture. It has no server of its own: data moves only between the student's computer and Google.
